Privacy Policy — Gateway Connector for Claude

Last updated: October 14, 2026

What the extension stores

The extension stores the following data locally in your browser:

  • Your gateway host (the domain of your own gateway server)
  • Your gateway username and password

Where this data goes

These credentials are sent only to the gateway server that you configure yourself, to authenticate your requests. They are never transmitted to the extension's developer or any third party.

What the extension does not do

  • No analytics, tracking, or telemetry of any kind
  • No data collection by the developer — the developer receives nothing
  • No browsing history, page content, or personal data is read or stored
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties

Permissions

  • declarativeNetRequest — routes requests to claude.ai and its asset domains to your configured gateway and adds your credentials to requests to that host.
  • scripting — applies interface settings (UTC time zone, en-US language) and privacy restrictions on your gateway pages only. The script is bundled in the extension.
  • storage — persists your gateway host and credentials locally in the browser.
  • proxy — sends document-preview connections through your gateway. No other sites are proxied.
  • webRequest — notices when the browser asks for the gateway proxy login. Page contents are not read.
  • webRequestAuthProvider — submits the gateway username and password you saved, only to your own gateway.
